# Master Plan: ZenPlant AAA Polish & Autonomous Local ML Evolution

## Goal
Transform ZenPlant into a visually stunning, emotionally resonant, and biologically accurate bonsai cultivation and zen pruning experience by leveraging local hardware resources (AMD Ryzen AI multi-core processing, local canvas shaders, spatial audio, and deep evolutionary machine learning).

## Phase 1: Visual & Atmospheric Elevation (Shaders, Lighting, & Materials)
- **Ambient Occlusion & Volumetric Lighting**: Implement multi-pass soft shadows and light rays emanating from behind the bonsai canopy.
- **Bark & Leaf Sub-surface Scattering**: Add procedural normal maps and organic color variance across leaf clusters and fissured bark surfaces.
- **Atmospheric Particles**: Falling pollen, floating cherry blossom petals (sakura), and dust motes caught in volumetric light shafts.

## Phase 2: Spatial Audio & Tactile Haptics
- **Procedural Soundscape**: Web Audio API generated binaural wind whispers, tactile pruning snips, gentle wood creaks, and water droplet tones.
- **Responsive Interactions**: Dynamic camera inertia and smooth zoom easing.

## Phase 3: Deep Local AI & Generational Bonsai Breeding
- **Species-Specific Neural Weights**: Pre-trained weight profiles for Japanese Bonsai, Zen Bamboo, and Sakura Orchid.
- **Autonomous Greenhouse Assistant**: Local AI that grades your pruning artistry and suggests aesthetic styling tips based on classical bonsai rules (shokan, kengai, broom style).
